A timeline of Warhammer's Old World

Since October 2026

I never found a clear timeline of the Warhammer Fantasy and The Old World setting online. Dates are scattered across dozens of books and wikis, and they don't always agree.

So I made one, from the Old Ones to the Storm of Chaos, filterable by faction and region. Each event cites its sources and flags where they disagree. The timeline itself is in French.

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON data. Code on GitHub

Lumen, widgets for the Windows desktop

Since October 2026

Lumen puts widgets on the Windows desktop: clock, weather, tasks, Pomodoro timer, GitHub activity, Obsidian notes, iCal or Google Calendar, YouTube videos and shortcuts.

It lives in the notification area and starts with Windows. Widgets can be placed freely or on a grid, and the look follows the chosen theme, or the time of day.

VIE Tracker, keeping watch on international volunteering offers

Since August 2026

VIE Tracker collects the V.I.E and V.I.A offers published online every day and scores them out of 20 on eight weighted criteria: job, country, allowance adjusted for cost of living, duration, company, freshness, competition and remote work.

It then tracks applications on a world map, a timeline of departures and statistics. An AI can also score offers through a built-in MCP server.

Python, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Model Context Protocol
